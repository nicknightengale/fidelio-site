Around Town Affiliate Terms

The terms for stores in Around Town

Version of November 6, 2026.

What this is

These terms are between Fidelio Cinema LLC (“Fidelio”, “we”) and the store that agrees to them (“you”). They make you a Fidelio Around Town Affiliate: your store gets a fuller page in Around Town, and your collection can show on the editions you carry.

Everything in these terms is free, both ways. Nobody pays anybody.

You agree by replying from your store’s email address with “I agree for [Store]”. We’ll each keep a PDF copy of these terms as they stood that day. The person who replies confirms they can agree for the store.

Around Town lists every store we can confirm is open, Affiliate or not. Being an Affiliate changes what your page shows, not whether you’re listed.

What we show

  • Your page. Name, address, hours, phone, website, and whether you rent, sell or both. These come from public sources and from you.
  • Your kind. A few words for what your store is, such as “Video store and cinema”. We fit them to how you describe yourself, and you approve them, as you do About.
  • About. A few lines about your store. We draft them from your own website, and nothing goes live until you approve the words.
  • Your photo. You send us a photo of your store and confirm you have the right to share it. You let us show it, free and non-exclusively, in the Fidelio app, on fidelio.film, and in Fidelio’s own marketing (social posts, our press page, App Store screenshots), always credited “Photo courtesy of [Store].” We stop using it when you ask or when these terms end.
  • What’s on. Your screenings and events. With your OK here, we read them from your own website or public calendar. You can also email them to stores@fidelio.film.
  • Your collection. Editions you carry show on their Fidelio pages as “In the collection at [Store]” (to rent) or “For sale at [Store]” (to buy), with the date of your latest export. See Your inventory export.

Your inventory export

Once a month, you email us an export of the discs you carry: a spreadsheet or file from your inventory system is fine.

  • What’s in it: each disc’s barcode, title and format, and whether it’s to rent or to buy. Add a sale price if you want it shown.
  • What’s never in it: rental history, customers, or who has what out. If a file arrives with any of that, we delete those columns before reading it.
  • What shows: “In the collection at [Store]” or “For sale at [Store]” on each matching edition, with the export’s date (“as of Oct 12”). We never say a disc is on the shelf today. Your collection stays on your listings even if an update is late; the date tells members how fresh it is.
  • Matching: we match your export to Fidelio’s editions and send you a list of the discs we couldn’t place.
  • What we keep from it: your latest export, while you’re an Affiliate. The facts it confirms about a disc (that a barcode is a particular edition, with its title and format) become part of Fidelio’s catalog, with no link to your store.
  • When you leave: we delete your exports and take your collection off every edition within 2 business days. The catalog facts stay.

What you get

  • Fidelio for your staff. Free Fidelio House Memberships for up to 3 staff households while you’re an Affiliate.
  • An offer for Fidelio House Members, if you want one. You may offer Fidelio House Members something at your store, such as a rental discount. They claim it by showing their member card in the Fidelio app. You set the offer and can change or end it any time; we show it on your page, in words you approve. Fidelio pays nothing toward it and takes nothing from it.
  • Founding Affiliate. Stores that agree by March 31, 2027 carry “Founding Affiliate” on their page for as long as they stay Affiliates.
  • Catalog matching. Each export, the list of discs we couldn’t match (see Your inventory export).

Fixes, takedowns and ending

  • Fixes and takedowns. Email stores@fidelio.film to correct anything on your page or take anything down: hours, words, a photo, an event, your collection. We do it within 2 business days.
  • Ending. Either of us can end these terms at any time by email, for any reason. Within 2 business days we take down your photo, About, What’s on, member offer and collection, and delete your exports. Your staff memberships end at their next renewal.
  • After ending. Your store stays in Around Town as a plain listing (name, address, hours), like every other open store, unless you ask us to remove it altogether. The catalog facts described under Your inventory export stay.

Everything else

  • Anything more. If Fidelio ever offers stores anything beyond these terms, it’ll be separate, and taking it is up to you.
  • Your store, your customers. You run your store and deal with your own customers. Fidelio isn’t part of any rental or sale.
  • Our app, our call. We decide how Fidelio looks and works, and we may change or stop Around Town. If we stop it, these terms end and we delete your exports.
  • Responsibility. Fidelio offers Around Town as it is, with no promises beyond what these terms say. Fidelio isn’t responsible to you for lost business or other indirect losses, and its total responsibility to you under these terms is at most $100.
  • Your contact’s details. We keep the name and email of the person we deal with at your store, as our Privacy Policy describes.
  • Changes. If we change these terms, we’ll email you at least 30 days ahead. Keep being an Affiliate after that and the new terms apply; if you’d rather not, end it by email.
  • Law. California law governs these terms, and any dispute is settled in the state or federal courts in Los Angeles County, California.
