Changes to the Terms and Privacy Policy

Every version of our Terms and Privacy Policy, newest first, with what changed and whether the change is big or small. Members are told in the app at least 7 days before a change takes effect; after a big change, the app asks you to agree again.

December 8, 2026 · Big change

Posted November 12, 2026. Takes effect December 8, 2026; that day the app asks everyone to agree again.

  • Paying through Fidelio. Second Run buyers pay in the app, and Fidelio takes the payment for the seller, as their agent. (Terms, Second Run.)
  • Buyer protection. A refund when a copy never arrives, isn’t as described or is a bootleg; tapping It arrived ends the right to report a problem. (Terms, Second Run.)
  • Seller Terms and fees. Sellers agree to the Second Run Seller Terms when they set up payouts; the rates are at fidelio.film/fees. (Seller Terms; Fees.)
  • Stripe. Stripe processes payments, refunds and payouts; Fidelio never sees card numbers or bank details. (Privacy Policy, What we don’t do and Service providers.)
  • Order records. Each order’s record is kept 7 years, a buyer’s address about 6 months after delivery, and “Something’s wrong” notes and photos 180 days after the report closes. (Privacy Policy, What we collect, Who sees it and How long we keep it.)
  • Deleting your account with open orders. The app first lists what happens to each open order; unshipped orders are cancelled and refunded. (Terms, Second Run; Privacy Policy, Deleting your account.)

November 15, 2026 · Big change

Posted November 6, 2026. Takes effect November 15, 2026 for members who agreed to the version of November 3; anyone agreeing after November 6 agrees to this version. First posted to take effect November 16; on November 13 it was moved a day earlier and the Lobby was added, as part of this big change.

  • The Lobby. Members can post photos of their shelves, setups and stacks, the discs they’ve just bought, photos from showings and their programmes, and reply to each other’s posts. You choose who sees each post: everyone in Fidelio, your Film Societies or your household; a showing photo starts at your Film Societies. A Just Bought post never shows a price, store or Shelf Number. Photos have their location and camera details removed. Every post and reply has Report and Block, reviewed within 24 hours; a block hides each side’s posts from the other, and from the blocker’s household. Posts and replies are kept until deleted or taken down, and go with your account. Added November 13, 2026, as part of this big change. (Terms, What you add to Fidelio and The Lobby; Privacy Policy, What we collect, Who sees it and How long we keep it.)
  • Reports and takedowns. Members can report a profile (its photo, name or something on its shelf), a single message, and Lobby posts and replies. Fidelio may clear a reported first name or take down a reported photo or Liner note, and tells you when something of yours is taken down after a report, never who reported it. The person reviewing a report sees what other members see: a first name, profile photo and region. Fidelio has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive members. If you leave a society, the photos you added to its showing albums stay; to remove them, delete them first or write to us. Added November 13, 2026, as part of this big change. (Terms, Messages and Rules for everyone; Privacy Policy, Reports and blocks, Who sees it and Showings.)
  • Lending discs. Members of a Film Society can lend each other discs, and keep a record of discs lent to someone outside Fidelio. Loans are between the two people and free: Fidelio keeps the record only and isn’t responsible for a lost, damaged or late disc. Lending and borrowing are for people 18 and over, confirmed once with the same tick as Second Run selling. We keep loan requests for 90 days after they close and loans for 2 years after they end; a name you type for someone outside Fidelio is kept only in your record. (Terms, Who can use Fidelio and Households and Film Societies; Privacy Policy, What we collect, Who sees it and How long we keep it.)
  • Showings. Members of a Film Society can post a showing of a disc, with a time, a place and seats, and members and guests invited by link can take a seat. Showings are free, private and 18+, and Fidelio isn’t responsible for what happens at one. Hosts can add co-hosts, a list of refreshments with who’s bringing each, and a waitlist when seats run out, and the people who went can share photos in the showing’s album (location and camera details removed; anyone in a photo can have it taken down). Outdoor showings show a forecast from Apple Weather, which gets only the showing’s area. We keep each showing, its optional address and parking note, its refreshments, album and who took or waited for a seat while the society lasts; the address and parking note are shown only to the hosts and the people with a seat, and are deleted if the showing is cancelled. A report of a showing keeps a copy of it without the address. A guest who isn’t a member gives only a first name, deleted 30 days after the showing. (Terms, Who can use Fidelio and Households and Film Societies; Privacy Policy, What we collect, Who sees it and How long we keep it.)
  • Liner notes and your room. You can write Liner notes about editions you own, seen on your profile and by your Film Societies, and add photos of your room and shelves (location and camera details removed) and the equipment you use, seen as your Shelf settings allow. (Privacy Policy, What you keep in Fidelio and Who sees it.)
  • Profile photo. You can add a photo of yourself, seen wherever other members see your first name (location and camera details removed), and change or remove it in Account. Members can report a photo; if we take yours down, you can choose another. (Privacy Policy, What you tell us about yourself, Who sees it and Reports.)
  • Film Society chats and the household thread. Each Film Society has a group chat, seen by its members from the day they joined; what you wrote stays under your first name if you leave, and the chat is kept while the society lasts. The two members of a household have their own thread, seen only by them. (Terms, Messages; Privacy Policy, Messages, Who sees it and How long we keep it.)
  • Last name. You can now add a last name, or let Sign in with Apple fill in your name the first time you sign in. Only you see your last name; other members see only its first letter, and only to tell apart two people with the same first name. (Privacy Policy, Your account, What you tell us about yourself and Who sees it.)
  • Barcodes we don’t know yet. When a barcode you scan or import isn’t in our catalog, we keep its number, the first and last day it came up and how often, never who sent it, until we add the disc (at most five years). (Privacy Policy, What we collect.)
  • Provisional editions. Some editions come from older records we haven’t checked yet; they’re marked provisional until we do. (Terms, The information in Fidelio.)
  • Stores in Around Town. Stores that join as Around Town Affiliates send us their contact’s name and email and files of the discs they carry, never rental history or customers. We delete the files when a store leaves, and the contact’s details within 30 days. The stores’ own terms are at fidelio.film/around-town-terms. (Privacy Policy, What we collect and How long we keep it.)

November 3, 2026 · Big change

Posted October 27, 2026. Takes effect November 3, 2026 for members who agreed to the version of October 27; anyone agreeing after October 27 agrees to this version.

  • If Fidelio changes hands. If Fidelio Cinema LLC is sold, merges or transfers Fidelio, your information and these Terms go to the new owner, who must keep to the Privacy Policy. We tell members in the app first. A company considering buying Fidelio may see member information only under a confidentiality agreement. (Privacy Policy, Who sees it; Terms, Ending.)
  • Signed-in devices. The IP address and device type kept for each device you’re signed in on are now removed no more than 90 days after you last use the app there, at once when you sign out, and all together when you delete your account. Records of a sign-in still in progress are kept for at most two days. (Privacy Policy, Technical details.)
  • Mailing address. The Contact sections now give Fidelio Cinema LLC’s mailing address: 2108 N St #17988, Sacramento, CA 95816. Added October 28, 2026; a small change that affects no one’s rights. (Terms and Privacy Policy, Contact.)
  • Fidelio House Membership. The season ticket is now Fidelio House Membership, a yearly subscription. The founding price is offered to members who join by 11:59 p.m. Pacific time on March 31, 2027, to members invited from our waitlist who signed up by then, and to a member who leaves a founding household and starts their own, and is kept for as long as the membership renews. Invitations record whether they carry the founding price. The 14-day free trial is offered in the app once per household, and a membership bought in your Apple account settings keeps the price and free days Apple gave. Added October 28, 2026; a small change that affects no one’s rights, since no membership had been sold. (Terms, Fidelio House Membership and other mentions; Privacy Policy, What we collect and Your invitation, How we use it, Who sees it.)
  • Apple Maps. Around Town’s small maps of stores come from Apple Maps, so Apple sees the area each map shows; we don’t send Apple your location or your account. Apple Maps is now named among our service providers. Added October 29, 2026; a small change that affects no one’s rights. (Privacy Policy, What stays on your phone and Service providers.)
  • Three records we now keep. So we never tell you the same thing twice, we keep a record of what each round-up told you, with any price shown, for 14 days. If you leave a household that has the founding price, we keep that you did, so a household you start later can join at that price, until you delete your account. If you suggest a correction to a store in Around Town, we keep it for 90 days, and who sent it for 30. The first two were added October 28, 2026 and the third October 29, 2026, as part of this big change. (Privacy Policy, Notifications, Fidelio House Membership and Store corrections.)
