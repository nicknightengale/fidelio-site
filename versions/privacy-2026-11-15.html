Privacy

What Fidelio keeps, and why

Effective November 15, 2026. Posted November 6, 2026; the Lobby added November 13, 2026. If you agreed to the version of November 3, 2026, it applies to you until November 15; if you agree after November 6, you agree to this version. What changed.

This policy covers the Fidelio app for iPhone (on the App Store as Fidelio Cinema) and fidelio.film, including its waitlist. Fidelio is published by Fidelio Cinema LLC.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in to the app with Apple. Apple gives us an email address: your own, or a private address that forwards to you if you choose Hide My Email. When you first sign in, Apple may offer to share the name on your Apple account. You can change it or leave it out before you agree, and if you share it, we use it only to fill in your first and last name, which you can change or clear in Account. We never see your Apple password.

What you tell us about yourself. If you choose, a first name, a last name and a region, in your own words, and a profile photo. Each is optional, and you can change or clear it in Account. Before your photo leaves your phone, its location and camera details are removed. Your region is only what you type; your phone’s location is never sent.

What you keep in Fidelio. Your Library (the editions you own, their Shelf Numbers, and the price you paid and the date you bought them, when you enter them), your wishlist, the programmes you make, your Lobby Cards and the answers on your profile, the labels you follow, your stores and your settings, including who sees your shelf. Also, if you write them, your Liner notes (what you say about an edition you own), and, if you add them, photos of your room and shelves (up to six) with their captions and the equipment you list (player, display, projector, sound). Before a photo leaves your phone, its location and camera details are removed.

Messages. The words and photos you send other members, in a conversation, your household’s thread or a Film Society’s chat, who sent them and when, and when you last read each one. Before a photo leaves your phone, its location and camera details are removed.

The Lobby. If you post in the Lobby, we keep the photo, what kind of post it is, your caption, the editions you tag, who you chose to see it, and when you posted it; and the replies you write, with when. Before a photo leaves your phone, its location and camera details are removed. A Just Bought post keeps only the edition, never its Shelf Number, price paid or store. If you post a programme or a photo from a showing’s album, we keep that it’s in the Lobby and who sees it. We also keep your Lobby setting (who sees your posts) and whether Fidelio offers Just Bought posts.

Film Societies. The societies you start or join, when you joined, and each society’s history lines (who joined, left or renamed it), which carry the first name you had at the time.

Loans. If you ask to borrow a disc, or lend one, in a Film Society, we keep the request, with its note if you add one (up to 280 characters), and how it was answered: lent, not now, withdrawn or expired. For each loan we keep the edition, which household’s copy it is and who lent it, who borrowed it, when it was lent, any due date and each change to it, and how and when it ended (returned, and on whose side it was marked; declined; cancelled; or ended because an account was deleted), including a return that was undone. The request and any due-date changes also appear in your conversation, kept like other messages. If you lend to someone outside Fidelio, or the other member has deleted their account, you can type their name (up to 40 characters); it’s kept only in your own record. We also keep your Lending and “Societies see your loans” settings, and whether you get due-date notifications.

Second Run. The copies you list: their photos, note, condition, price, shipping cost, whether you ship or offer pickup, and when each was listed, updated and sold.

Reports and blocks. If you report a conversation, a listing, a showing, a member’s profile (their photo, name or something on their shelf), a photo, or a Lobby post or reply, we keep the reason, your note if you add one, and a copy of what was reported: for a conversation, its last 50 messages with their photos, marked only by which side wrote each (in a Film Society’s chat, which member); for a single message, that message and the two before it; for a showing, its films, place name, time, note and refreshments, never its address; for a photo, the photo; for a Lobby post, its photo, caption and tagged editions; for a reply, its words; for a profile, the photo, name or shelf item reported. If you block someone, we keep that you did, and when.

Store corrections. If you suggest a correction to a store in Around Town, we keep the store, what kind of correction it is, your note if you add one, when you sent it, and that it came from you.

Barcodes we don’t know yet. When a barcode isn’t in our catalog yet, we keep its number, the first and last day it was looked up, and how many times it was scanned or appeared in an import, so we can add that disc. We never keep who scanned or imported it. We keep this until the disc is added, for at most five years from the first day.

Showings. If you post a showing in a Film Society, we keep the film or films, the date and time, the place as you name it, its address and a note on parking and directions if you add them, the number of seats, whose disc it is, whether it’s outdoors, any co-hosts, the refreshments listed and who’s bringing each, and your note. If you take a seat or join the waitlist for one, we keep that you did, and when. If you add photos to a showing’s album, we keep them; before a photo leaves your phone, its location and camera details are removed.

Guests at a showing. If someone invites you to a showing by link and you aren’t a member, you take a seat on fidelio.film with a first name and confirm you’re 18 or over. We keep that name with your seat. You’re welcome to download Fidelio and join, but you don’t have to.

Your agreements. When you agreed to our Terms, and which version, and, if you confirm it, that you’re 18 or over (asked once, for selling in Second Run, for loans and for showings).

Fidelio House Membership. If your household buys a membership, Apple tells us which kind it is, when it starts, renews or ends, and whether it was refunded. We read this directly from Apple, with no other billing company in between, and keep it with your household’s records, including our backups. We never see your payment details.

Notifications. If you turn on notifications, Apple gives us a token for your iPhone so we can send them, and we keep the time you chose for your daily round-up and your time zone. So we never tell you the same thing twice, we keep a record of what each round-up told you, with any price shown, for 14 days.

Your invitation. Fidelio is by invitation. When you enter an invite code, we record that it admitted you and whether it carries the founding price. We keep only a fingerprint of each code, never the code itself. Film Society invitation links are kept the same way.

Stores in Around Town. When a store becomes an Around Town Affiliate, we keep the name and email of the person we deal with there, the store’s photo and the words it approves, its events, and the inventory files it sends us: each disc’s barcode, title and format, whether it’s to rent or to buy, and a sale price if the store chooses. These files never include rental history or customers. Only Fidelio sees a store contact’s details. The store’s own terms are at fidelio.film/around-town-terms.

The waitlist. On fidelio.film, the waitlist keeps the email address you enter, the labels and devices you choose, and when you joined.

Technical details. For each device you’re signed in on, we keep its IP address and device type while you stay signed in on it, and for no more than 90 days after you last use the app there. Signing out removes it at once, and deleting your account removes all of them. Records of a sign-in that’s still in progress are kept for at most two days. The company that hosts our database also logs IP addresses, for 7 days, and GitHub, which hosts this website, logs visits under its own policy. We use these only to run and protect Fidelio.

What stays on your phone

Location. Around Town can use your location to show stores in your city. It is worked out on your phone, at city level, and is never stored or sent to us. The small maps of stores come from Apple Maps, so Apple sees the area each map shows, under its own privacy policy. We don’t send Apple your location or your account.

Searches. What you search for is sent only to find editions, and isn’t kept with your account.

The barcode scanner. It reads barcodes on your phone. Only the barcode’s number is sent, to find the edition, and the scanner’s pictures are never saved or sent. Photos are sent only when you choose to send one in a message, add a profile photo, add one to a listing, add photos of your room and shelves, add one to a showing’s album, or post one in the Lobby.

What we don’t do

Fidelio carries no advertising. We don’t sell or rent your information, share it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The app has no advertising or analytics trackers. The website sets no cookies and uses no outside scripts or fonts, and no other company tracks visitors across websites through it. It behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, because there is no such tracking to turn off.

How we use it

To run Fidelio for you: keeping your Library and wishlist, showing prices for the editions you want, delivering your messages, running your Film Societies, Lobby posts and Second Run listings, sending the notifications and emails you choose, reviewing reports, and answering you when you write. On the waitlist, to send your invitation and news of the opening, to keep the founding price for you, and to decide which labels to cover first.

The Digest. Members get the Digest, an email about the editions, labels and reviews they follow. It comes weekly unless you choose daily or off, in the app or from the links in any Digest; those links work without signing in, and one of them stops all our marketing email. Each Digest names Fidelio Cinema LLC and carries our mailing address. It goes to your account’s email, including a Hide My Email address if you chose one.

Who sees it

Your last name is for you. Other members see your first name, with your profile photo if you add one, and in a Film Society where two members share a first name, the first letter of your last name too.

Your household. Members of your household share its Library, wishlist and programmes, and see each other’s Lobby Cards. Copies you add carry your first name, if you’ve given one. If your household turns on Shared inbox, your household-mates can read your conversations too, including messages other members send you.

Your shelf. Who sees your shelf beyond your household is your choice in Shelf settings: everyone on Fidelio, your Film Societies, or only your household. Shelf Numbers, the prices you paid and the dates you bought are never shown outside your household.

Film Societies. People in a society see its members’ first names, its history lines, and the shelves of members who chose to show theirs to it. A loan is seen by the members of the two households in it, and by your Film Societies only if you turn on “Societies see your loans”; a name you type for a borrower or lender is seen only by you.

Members you write to. They see your first name and what you send, and so do their household-mates if their household turns on Shared inbox.

Chats. A Film Society’s chat is seen by its members, each from the day they joined, with every writer’s first name. If you leave a society, what you wrote in its chat stays under your first name. Your household’s thread is seen only by the two of you.

Second Run. When you list a copy, members who view the listing see your first name, your region, when you joined Fidelio, the Lobby Card you choose for your listings (even if it’s hidden on your shelf), your shelf as your Shelf settings allow, and the listing itself. In conversations about a listing, they also see the price it was first listed at. A household-mate’s name is never shown to buyers.

Showings. A showing is seen by the members of its Film Society and by the guests invited to it, and so are who has a seat or is waiting for one (guests’ first names included), its refreshments and who’s bringing them, and its photo album. Its address and parking note are shown only to its hosts and to the people with a seat. You can delete photos you added, a host can remove any photo from their showing’s album, and if you’re in a photo you’d rather not be in, report it in the app or write to support@fidelio.film and we’ll take it down. If you leave a society, the photos you added to its showing albums stay there under your first name; to remove them, delete them before you leave or write to support@fidelio.film.

Liner notes, your room and your shelves. Your Liner notes are seen on your profile and by your Film Societies. Photos of your room and shelves, and your equipment, are seen by whoever your Shelf settings let see your shelf.

Lobby Cards. Your household sees them. A card you share yourself goes wherever you send it, and the card you choose for your listings is seen by members viewing them.

The Lobby. Each post is seen by everyone in Fidelio, your Film Societies or your household, as you choose in Your shelf and privacy or for that post; a photo you post from a showing’s album starts at your Film Societies. Others see your first name and profile photo with it. Replies are seen by whoever can see the post. A Just Bought post shows the edition and its cover, never the price you paid, the store or its Shelf Number. Your programmes stay private until you post one. If you block someone, you and your household won’t see their posts, replies or Lobby Cards in the Lobby, and they won’t see yours.

Reports. A report goes to Fidelio, never to the member reported, and nobody is told who reported. A person at Fidelio reviews each one within 24 hours, every day, and sees the reported member’s first name, profile photo and region, as other members do. If something of yours is taken down after a report, we tell you, never who reported it. Each report is emailed to support@fidelio.film with only its kind, reason, time and a reference number: no message text, notes, names or email addresses.

Store corrections. Only Fidelio reads them, to fix the store’s details. Other members and the store never see them, or who sent them.

Service providers. Each handles your information only to provide its service to Fidelio, and is bound to protect it at least as well as this policy does:

  • Supabase hosts Fidelio’s database, sign-in, and the photos in messages, listings, profiles, showing albums and Lobby posts.
  • DigitalOcean runs Fidelio’s servers (from November 2026).
  • Backblaze holds encrypted backup copies (from November 2026).
  • Resend sends Fidelio’s email.
  • GitHub hosts fidelio.film.
  • Anthropic provides the Claude AI tools that help us run and check Fidelio’s systems, and may process member information when we do.
  • Apple runs Sign in with Apple, the App Store, membership purchases and notifications, draws the maps in Around Town (Apple Maps), gives the forecast for outdoor showings (Apple Weather, sent only the showing’s area, never your account), and hosts our support mailbox (iCloud Mail). If you test Fidelio through TestFlight, Apple passes us the feedback and crash reports you choose to send.

Stores. Some store links are affiliate links. If you follow one, the store and its affiliate network may record that the visit came from Fidelio, under their own policies. What you do at the store is between you and the store. The store or its network may tell us that a purchase was made and what commission we earned, but never your name or payment details. More on how Fidelio is paid is on the About page.

The law. We share information if the law requires it, or to protect Fidelio’s members or Fidelio Cinema LLC.

If Fidelio changes hands. If Fidelio Cinema LLC is sold, merges with another company, or transfers Fidelio to another company, your information goes with Fidelio to the new owner, who must keep to this policy. Any change to it after that follows the Changes section below. We tell members in the app before such a transfer happens. A company considering buying Fidelio may see member information only under a confidentiality agreement, and only as far as it needs to.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account, with these exceptions.

Messages are kept while either member in the conversation still has an account. If you delete a message, its words and photo are removed from the conversation at once, leaving a line that says a message was deleted. A Film Society’s chat is kept while the society lasts, and your household’s thread while either of you still has an account.

Lobby posts and replies are kept until you delete them, we take them down, or you delete your account. Deleting a post deletes its replies; removing a photo from a showing’s album removes its Lobby post.

Reports are kept until they’re answered, then for 1 year, and deleted with any photos only they still held. Report emails are deleted from our mailbox after 1 year.

Store corrections are kept for 90 days. The record of who sent one is removed after 30 days.

Film Societies. If you leave a society, your first name stays in its history lines while the society lasts. Invitation links are deleted 30 days after they expire. A society ends, with its history, when its last member leaves.

Loans. A request, with its note, is deleted 90 days after it closes. A loan is kept while it’s open, and deleted 2 years after it ends.

Showings are kept, with their addresses, parking notes, refreshments, photo albums and who took or waited for a seat, while their Film Society lasts, unless the host deletes one or a photo is removed sooner. If a host cancels a showing, its address and parking note are deleted at once. A guest’s first name is deleted 30 days after the showing.

Stores in Around Town. When a store stops being an Around Town Affiliate, we delete its inventory files within 2 business days, and its contact’s name and email within 30 days. Facts a file confirmed about a disc (that a barcode is a particular edition, with its title and format) stay in our catalog, without the store’s name.

Backups. We back up members’ information every night and delete each backup after 28 days. Copies stored off our own equipment are encrypted before they leave it.

Deleting your account. In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. When you delete your account, we delete it from our systems straight away, and any copies in our backups are deleted within 30 days. Your sign-in, name, profile photo, region, followed labels, settings, stores, blocks, store corrections and your record of agreeing to the Terms go with it (except a record the law requires us to keep, for as long as it does), and your email is removed from the waitlist. Messages you sent stay in the conversations and chats you sent them to, signed “Former member”; your photos in them are deleted, except a photo a kept report still holds, which goes when that report’s year is up. Showings you posted lose their address and parking note and show “Former member” as host, and any still to come are cancelled; your name comes off any showing’s refreshments. Your Liner notes, room photos, the photos you added to showing albums, and your Lobby posts and replies are deleted. Your loans end, and the other member’s record shows “Former member” unless they type a name. Your first name in Film Society history lines becomes “Former member”, and in a society you looked after, the member who joined earliest takes over. If you share a household, the copies and wishes you added stay in its Library without your name. If you were the only member of your household, we keep its Library for 30 days in case you change your mind, then delete it; any backup copies of it are deleted within 7 days after that. To keep it, write to support@fidelio.film within those 30 days, from the same email address or by replying to any email we’ve sent you. Your account itself can’t be brought back.

To stop Sign in with Apple for Fidelio as well, open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security, and remove Fidelio.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold, or to leave the waitlist, write to support@fidelio.film. We answer within 30 days. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your iPhone’s Settings, change or stop the Digest from its links, and reply to any email from Fidelio to stop receiving more.

Fidelio is for people 13 and over, and Second Run selling, Film Society loans and showings for people 18 and over. Fidelio is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We post every new version of this policy on this page with its date and a short summary of what changed. We tell members in the app at least 7 days before a change takes effect. If a change affects what we collect, what we share or with whom, or who sees it, the app asks you to agree again on the day it takes effect.
