About

A record of the discs you own, and the ones you want

Fidelio is an iPhone app, with a web companion, for people who collect films on disc. Every title in it is an exact edition: the label, the format, the packaging, the barcode. Members keep their collection and wishlist by edition, and every edition has its own page, showing what’s in it and which stores sell it.

The catalog begins with the boutique labels collectors care most about, including Criterion, Kino Lorber, Arrow, A24 and Shout Factory, and grows label by label from there.

Fidelio is free to keep your collection, with an optional Fidelio House Membership. It carries no advertising. It opens first to invited members; the waitlist is on the front page. What Fidelio is.

How Fidelio is paid

Fidelio will be funded by its members. Some links from an edition page to a store will be affiliate links: if you buy through one, the store may pay Fidelio a small commission, at no extra cost to you. Retailers do not pay to be listed, and a commission never changes which editions Fidelio shows, the order they appear in, or what Fidelio says about them.
