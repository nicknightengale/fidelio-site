Fidelio

About

A record of the discs you own, and the ones you want

Fidelio is an iPhone app, with a web companion, for people who collect films on disc. Every title in it is an exact edition: the label, the format, the packaging, the barcode. Members keep their collection and wishlist by edition, and Fidelio tells them when a wishlisted edition goes on sale, reaches the lowest price we have seen, or opens for preorder as a limited edition.

The catalog begins with the boutique labels collectors care most about, including Criterion, Kino Lorber, Arrow, A24 and Shout Factory, and grows label by label from there.

Fidelio will be a paid membership with no advertising. It opens to invited members in late 2026; the waitlist is on the front page.

How Fidelio is paid

Fidelio will be funded by its members. Some links from an edition page to a store will be affiliate links: if you buy through one, the store may pay Fidelio a small commission, at no extra cost to you. Retailers do not pay to be listed, and a commission never changes which editions Fidelio shows, the order they appear in, or the prices and alerts you see.
