Second Run Seller Terms

Version of December 8, 2026. Effective December 8, 2026. Posted November 13, 2026. You agree to these when you set up payouts to sell in Second Run. What changed.

1. Who can sell, and how you start

These Seller Terms add to Fidelio’s Terms and Privacy Policy whenever you sell in Second Run. If they differ, these Seller Terms win for selling.

  • You must be 18 or over and live in the United States, with a US bank account in your own name or your business’s name.
  • To sell, you set up payouts with Stripe, the company that processes Second Run payments. Stripe checks who you are and holds your account under its own Connected Account Agreement. You agree to that too.
  • You agree to these Seller Terms when you finish setting up payouts. We record that you agreed, and when.

2. Fidelio is your payment agent

You appoint Fidelio Cinema LLC as your agent to receive buyers’ payments for your Second Run sales. Once a buyer pays Fidelio, they have paid you, and they owe you nothing more for that sale.

  • We hold the payment for you, through Stripe, and pass it on to you as these terms describe. We never hold it as our own money. The buyer’s card statement shows Fidelio, because we take the payment for you.
  • The sale is between you and the buyer. Fidelio isn’t the seller and doesn’t own your copy at any point.
  • We act as your agent only to receive payments, release them to you, and refund buyers when these terms say so. We can’t make any other promise on your behalf.

3. What you can list

List only copies you own, of official releases, described honestly.

  • No bootlegs, counterfeits, copied discs, recordable discs (BD-R, DVD-R) or home-made versions.
  • Describe the copy’s condition and everything that comes with it (slipcover, booklet, inserts, extra discs), and say what’s missing.
  • Your photos show the copy itself, not a stock picture.
  • The copy should be in your Fidelio Library. You can have up to 25 listings active at once.
  • A listing for a copy that isn’t in your Library, was added to it less than 30 days ago, or would take you past 25 waits for our review before anyone sees it. If you sell as a business, we’ll ask you to sell as a Concession, labelled as a shop.
  • Keep the sale in Fidelio. Don’t ask a buyer to pay you outside Fidelio for a copy you listed here, unless you hand it over in person (section 5).
  • We may remove any listing that breaks these terms or the main Terms.

4. Fees

Fidelio’s fee is shown before you list and again on every sale, and current rates are always at fidelio.film/fees.

  • The fee is taken from the sale (disc plus shipping) before we pay you. Buyers never see it.
  • A household with an annual Fidelio House Membership sells its first 12 sales each membership year with no Fidelio fee. The count starts again at each annual renewal, and belongs to the household: if you join another household, you share whatever fee-free sales it has left.
  • Shops selling in Second Run (Concessions) pay the Concession rate. Stores that are Fidelio Around Town Affiliates pay the lower Around Town Concession rate. A Founding Around Town Affiliate also sells its first 12 sales each year with no Fidelio fee, counted from the day it agreed to the Around Town Affiliate Terms; other shops never get fee-free sales.
  • We’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice in the app before a rate goes up. A sale is always charged the rate shown when it was listed.

5. After a sale

Ship within 3 business days (Monday to Friday, not US federal holidays), with tracking, packed so the disc and case arrive as described.

  • Add the tracking number in the app, or buy a label in Fidelio, which adds it for you. The buyer’s shipping payment covers the label.
  • If you haven’t shipped within 3 business days, the buyer may cancel for a full refund.
  • If you haven’t added tracking 2 business days after the ship-by date, the order is cancelled and the buyer refunded in full. It counts as a cancellation, and the payment fee is charged to you.
  • If the carrier hasn’t scanned your tracking number 3 days after you add it, we’ll let you know, and you have 2 business days (not counting weekends or US federal holidays) to fix it. Changing the number doesn’t restart that time. If it still hasn’t been scanned by then, the order is cancelled and the buyer refunded in full.
  • If a package from a cancelled order reaches the buyer anyway, the disc is still yours. Work it out with the buyer in Messages: they can send it back at your cost, or buy it from you again in a new listing. The cancellation and the payment fee still stand.
  • If you can’t complete a sale (the copy is damaged, lost or already sold), cancel it in the app and the buyer is refunded at once. Three cancellations in 90 days pauses your selling while we take a look. A buyer cancelling because you didn’t ship in time counts as one, and so does an order cancelled because the carrier never scanned your tracking number and you didn’t fix it in time.
  • Use the buyer’s address only to send this order. It leaves your order screen once the payment is released to you or the order is cancelled.
  • Sales handed over in person aren’t paid through Fidelio; you and the buyer settle directly, as the main Terms describe.

6. Getting paid

Your money is released when the buyer says it arrived, or 3 days after tracking shows it delivered, whichever comes first.

  • If tracking never shows delivery, the money is released 21 days after you ship, unless the buyer reports it missing first.
  • Until it’s released, the buyer can tell us something’s wrong (section 7). The app shows you and the buyer the release date.
  • Once a sale is released to you, your money is paid out to your bank the same day. Banks usually show it within 2 business days.
  • What you receive is the sale price plus shipping, less any Fidelio fee and any label you bought in Fidelio. Each order shows the amount before it’s released.

7. Buyer protection, returns and lost parcels

Buyers are protected when a copy doesn’t match its listing, and the refund comes from that sale.

  • Not as described: a buyer may report it within 3 days of delivery. They send the copy back first, with a return label charged to you, and are refunded once it reaches you. Returns are arranged by Fidelio support at first.
  • Never arrived, or a bootleg: the buyer is refunded at once, without a return. Your first confirmed bootleg ends your selling in Second Run.
  • Changed their mind: not covered. Buyers can’t return a copy that matches its listing.
  • Lost in the mail: if tracking never shows delivery and the buyer reports it missing, the buyer is refunded from the sale. If you bought the label in Fidelio, it carries tracking and the carrier’s own cover, and we claim from the carrier for you. What the carrier pays, less the claim service’s share, is added to your next payout.
  • Fidelio decides these cases from the listing, the photos, tracking and what both of you tell us, and its decision on a refund is final in the app.

When a sale is refunded because you cancelled it, didn’t ship in time, gave a tracking number the carrier never scanned, or sent a copy that wasn’t as described or was a bootleg, you also pay the card processing fee on that sale, which the payment processor doesn’t return. The amount is shown in the order.

8. Chargebacks, and money you owe

If a buyer disputes a payment with their card company, we contest it using your evidence, and you pay only if the dispute is lost.

  • We’ll ask for tracking, photos and messages. Send them within 5 days so we can respond in time.
  • If the dispute is lost, you owe the amount refunded plus the dispute fees the payment processor charges (currently $15, plus $15 more when we contest it). If it’s won, you owe nothing.
  • Money you owe, from a lost dispute, a refund after you were paid, or a processing fee under section 7, comes out of your next payouts first.
  • If your payouts haven’t covered it within 30 days, your selling pauses until it’s paid, and we may collect it from the bank account linked to your payouts, through Stripe.
  • Disputes are handled by Fidelio support at first.

9. Pausing, stopping and closing

You can stop selling at any time; we can pause or end your selling if you break these terms.

  • To stop, remove your listings and finish any open sales. Close payouts in the app when you’re done.
  • We may pause your selling while we look into a report, end it for a bootleg or repeated problems, and remove your listings.
  • If your selling is paused or ended, or you close your account, we may hold money still owed to you for up to 90 days, to cover refunds and disputes on sales already made. Whatever isn’t needed is then paid to you.
  • If you delete your Fidelio account, orders you haven’t shipped are cancelled and the buyers refunded, and you pay the processing fee on each. Orders you’ve shipped finish as usual, and your money is paid out as they’re released. Money you owe on sales made before you deleted your account can still be collected as section 8 describes.
  • We’ll tell you in the app or by email why your selling was paused or ended, and you can reply to support@fidelio.film.

10. Taxes, and the rest

You handle the taxes on your own income from selling; Fidelio collects sales tax where the law makes Fidelio responsible for it.

  • Where the law requires, we report your sales to the IRS and your state on Form 1099-K, through Stripe, using the details you gave Stripe.
  • Buyers’ names and addresses are given to you only to complete their order. Don’t keep them, share them or contact buyers for anything else.
  • We may change these Seller Terms. For a change that affects your money or your duties, we’ll tell you in the app at least 7 days ahead and ask you to agree again before you list. A sale is always governed by the terms in place when it was made.
  • Everything else, including our limits on liability and how disputes with Fidelio are settled, is in the main Terms.
  • Questions: support@fidelio.film.
