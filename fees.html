Second Run fees

Rates from December 8, 2026. Posted November 13, 2026.

Buying in Second Run costs only the price and shipping the seller sets. Sellers pay Fidelio’s fee, a share of the sale (disc plus shipping) taken before they’re paid. The fee includes the card processing cost. The app shows it before you list and again on every sale.

Rates

  • Members selling their own copies: 9% of the sale.
  • Shops (Concessions): 10% of the sale.
  • Stores that are Fidelio Around Town Affiliates (Around Town Concessions): 6% of the sale.

Sales with no Fidelio fee

  • A household with an annual Fidelio House Membership sells its first 12 sales each membership year with no Fidelio fee. The count starts again at each annual renewal.
  • A Founding Around Town Affiliate sells its first 12 sales each year with no Fidelio fee, counted from the day it agreed to the Around Town Affiliate Terms.

An example

A member sells a $30 disc and charges $5 for shipping, at the 9% rate:

  • The sale: $35.00 (what the buyer pays).
  • Fidelio’s fee, 9%: $3.15.
  • The seller receives: $31.85, or $26.65 if they bought a $5.20 shipping label in Fidelio.
  • As one of their household’s fee-free sales, the seller receives $35.00, or $29.80 with the label.

Other charges

Besides the fee, a seller pays only for:

  • a shipping label, if they buy one in Fidelio;
  • the card processing fee on a sale refunded because of the seller (a cancellation, late shipping, a tracking number the carrier never scanned, or a copy that wasn’t as described or was a bootleg), shown in the order;
  • a lost bank dispute: the amount refunded plus the dispute fees the payment processor charges.

When rates change

We tell sellers in the app at least 30 days before a rate goes up, and a sale is always charged the rate shown when it was listed. The full rules for selling are in the Second Run Seller Terms.
