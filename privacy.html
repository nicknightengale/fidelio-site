Privacy

What this site keeps, and why

Effective October 9, 2026. This notice covers fidelio.film and its waitlist. The Fidelio app will have its own policy before it opens.

What we collect

When you request an invitation, we keep the email address you enter, the labels and device you choose (both optional), and the time of your request. That is all the waitlist collects.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To send your invitation, and occasional news about Fidelio's opening.
  • To decide which labels to cover first.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the waitlist.
  • Resend sends us a notice of each new request.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Affiliate links

Some store links on Fidelio may be affiliate links. If you follow one, the store and its affiliate network may record that the visit came from Fidelio, under their own privacy policies. See how Fidelio is paid.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to leave the waitlist, write to nick@fidelio.film. We act on requests within 30 days. Every email we send also lets you unsubscribe.

The waitlist is for adults; we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
