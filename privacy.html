Privacy

What Fidelio keeps, and why

Effective October 22, 2026. This policy covers the Fidelio app for iPhone and fidelio.film, including its waitlist. Fidelio is published by Fidelio Cinema LLC.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in to the app with Apple. Apple gives us an email address: your own, or a private address that forwards to you if you choose Hide My Email. We don’t ask Apple for your name, and we never see your Apple password.

What you tell us about yourself. If you choose, a first name, which your household sees beside the copies you add, and a region, in your own words. Both are optional, and you can change or clear them in Account. Your region is only what you type; your phone’s location is never sent.

What you keep in Fidelio. Your Library (the editions you own, their Shelf Numbers, and the price you paid and the date you bought them, when you enter them), your wishlist, the programmes you make, your Lobby Cards and the answers on your profile, the labels you follow, your stores and your settings.

Your invitation. Fidelio is by invitation. When you enter an invite code, we record that it admitted you. We keep only a fingerprint of each code, never the code itself.

The waitlist. On fidelio.film, the waitlist keeps the email address you enter, the labels and devices you choose, and when you joined.

What stays on your phone

Location. Around Town can use your location to show stores in your city. It is worked out on your phone, at city level, and is never stored or sent to us.

Searches. What you search for is sent only to find editions, and isn’t kept.

Camera. The barcode scanner reads barcodes on your phone. Only the barcode’s number is sent, to find the edition. Pictures are never saved or sent.

What we don’t do

Fidelio carries no advertising. We don’t sell or rent your information, share it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The app has no advertising or analytics trackers. The website sets no cookies, and behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

How we use it

To run Fidelio for you: keeping your Library and wishlist, showing prices for the editions you want, sending the alerts and emails you choose, and answering you when you write. On the waitlist, to send your invitation and news of the opening, and to decide which labels to cover first.

Who sees it

Your household. Members of your household share its Library, wishlist and programmes, and see each other’s Lobby Cards. Copies you add carry your first name, if you’ve given one. Lobby Cards are seen only by your household unless you share one yourself; a card you share goes wherever you send it.

Service providers. Companies that host Fidelio’s database, deliver the app and send its email handle your information only to provide those services.

Apple. Apple runs Sign in with Apple, the App Store and notifications. If you test Fidelio through TestFlight, Apple passes us the feedback and crash reports you choose to send.

Stores. Some store links are affiliate links. If you follow one, the store and its affiliate network may record that the visit came from Fidelio, under their own policies. What you do at the store is between you and the store; we don’t see what you buy there. More on how Fidelio is paid is on the About page.

The law. We share information if the law requires it, or to protect Fidelio’s members or Fidelio Cinema LLC.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account.

Deleting your account. In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. When you delete your account, we delete it from our systems straight away, and any copies in our backups are deleted within 30 days. Your sign-in, name, region, followed labels, settings and stores go with it, and your email is removed from the waitlist. If you share a household, the copies and wishes you added stay in its Library without your name. If you were the only member of your household, we keep its Library for 30 days in case you change your mind, then delete it; any backup copies of it are deleted within 7 days after that. To keep it, write to support@fidelio.film from the same email address within those 30 days. Your account itself can’t be brought back.

Fidelio also asks Apple to stop Sign in with Apple for Fidelio. If you skip that step, you can remove Fidelio yourself in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name, then Sign-In & Security.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold, or to leave the waitlist, write to support@fidelio.film. We answer within 30 days. You can turn alerts off in the app or in your iPhone’s Settings, and reply to any email from Fidelio to stop receiving more.

Fidelio is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We post any new version of this policy on this page with its date. If a change affects what we collect or who sees it, we tell members in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact

Write to support@fidelio.film.
