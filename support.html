Support

Write to us

Write to support@fidelio.film. A person reads every message, and we answer within two working days.

Signing in

Fidelio signs in with Apple only. If you chose Hide My Email, our emails reach you through Apple’s forwarding address.

Invitations

Fidelio is by invitation during its first season. If your code doesn’t work, write to us from the address your invitation went to.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. What goes and what stays is set out in our Privacy policy.

Images and rights

Questions about images or rights go to rights@fidelio.film.
